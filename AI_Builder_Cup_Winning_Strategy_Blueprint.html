4 The 5 Strategic Enhancements to Implement

1. Multimodal Appliance Diagnostic & Quoter

Feature: Customer snaps a photo of their broken appliance (water purifier, pump motor, AC).
Google AI: gemini-1.5-flash analyzes the image via structured JSON schema.
Output: Detected defect, severity level (LOW/MED/HIGH), necessary spare parts, and upfront fixed price.

2. Gemini Biometric KYC Anti-Spoofing

Feature: Dual-stage facial verification at the customer's doorstep.
Edge Layer: Instant HTML5 canvas face detection (0ms).
Google AI: Gemini 1.5 Flash compares the live selfie against the government KYC ID, generating a 90%+ match confidence and spoof flag for the Admin Audit.

3. Indic Multilingual Voice AI Assistant

Feature: Microphone button on search & booking bar.
Languages: Kannada (ಕನ್ನಡ), Hindi, English.
Google AI: Voice-to-intent parsing extracts the appliance issue, urgency, and address, pre-populating the booking in 1 tap for elderly/rural users.

4. Proximity & Pool Isolation Guard

Feature: Strictly enforces a 25km geofence.
Fraud Block: A technician in Bangalore cannot see, accept, or finish jobs in Sagara.
Pool Isolation: Once a job is accepted, public pool jobs are hidden until the job is completed or cancelled.

5. Admin Mission Control, Doorstep Audits & SOS Emergency Center

Customer Privacy vs Admin Audit: Customers only see verified technician badges; raw site photos and selfies are securely routed to the private doorstep_audits collection.
SOS Siren: Real-time audio siren in /admin with instant 112 emergency dialing and customer/technician telephony routing.

5 Step-by-Step Implementation Roadmap

  1. Step 1: Free Gemini API Key Setup — Acquire free API key from aistudio.google.com and add VITE_GEMINI_API_KEY to frontend/.env.
  2. Step 2: Create Resilient gemini.ts Engine — Build rate-limited client-side wrapper calling gemini-1.5-flash with structured JSON outputs and offline fallback.
  3. Step 3: Integrate Visual Diagnostic into Customer Booking — Add photo upload trigger with real-time AI fault breakdown and transparent parts pricing.
  4. Step 4: Connect Live Selfie to Gemini Biometric Face Match — Perform multimodal similarity comparison during technician doorstep arrival and record into Firestore doorstep_audits.
  5. Step 5: Voice Assistant (Web Speech + Gemini Intent) — Enable 1-tap Kannada/English voice intake on search bar.
  6. Step 6: Automated Vitest Test Suite Execution — Verify that all 18+ test suites pass with 100% coverage.
  7. Step 7: Production Build & Firebase Hosting Deploy — Run npm run build and npx firebase deploy --only hosting:myurbandaily.
